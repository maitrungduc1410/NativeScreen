## Description

Native Screen turns a plain screen recording into a polished demo: zooms that follow your clicks, a cursor that glides, captions, backgrounds, and a real multi-track timeline when you want control. Everything runs on your Mac.

ZOOMS THAT FOLLOW YOU
Clicks and typing become zooms on their own. Move them, change the depth, or switch to a loupe that magnifies one spot.

A CURSOR THAT GLIDES
Shaky paths are smoothed, the cursor can be resized or restyled after recording, and clicks get a quiet ripple.

SHORTCUTS PEOPLE CAN FOLLOW
The keyboard shortcuts you press show up as keycaps in the video, so viewers can repeat what you did.

DRAW WHILE YOU TALK
Press a key during a recording and draw on the screen with a pen, a highlighter, arrows or boxes. Every stroke lands in the editor as an annotation you can still change.

ANNOTATIONS THAT DRAW THEMSELVES IN
Add text, arrows, shapes and images on their own tracks. They draw in and fade out right on time.

YOUR CAMERA, YOUR WAY
Show yourself in a corner, side by side, or cut out from your background. Blur the room or swap it for a photo, and add a sticker that stays on your face as you move.

EDIT THE VIDEO BY EDITING THE WORDS
Captions are made on your Mac, in your language. The transcript becomes an editor: delete a sentence to cut it, or clear every filler word and long pause in one pass.

ONE RECORDING, EVERY SHAPE
Switch between wide, square and vertical at any time. Zooms re-aim and captions move to stay readable.

A TIMELINE WITH AS MANY TRACKS AS THE STORY NEEDS
Each kind of item gets its own tracks, and you can add more: two blurs that overlap, caption lines for two speakers, shortcuts on their own lane.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Recording, captions, sensitive data detection and export all run on your Mac. No account, no upload, no tracking.

CRASH SAFE
Recordings are written in small pieces as you go, so a crash or a dead battery does not lose the take.

OPEN FORMAT
Projects are folders with documented JSON, and exports are standard MP4, ProRes or GIF.

FREE, FOR GOOD
No trial, no plan, no watermark. Native Screen is open source. If it helps you, star the repository or send a fix.
